Clean Water Systems — Nyando

Our Nyando project installs locally operated water filters, builds household water storage units, and trains women’s groups to run the system.

Impact

Since launch, over 4,000 people now access safe drinking water and school attendance has improved in surrounding villages.

Next steps

We are expanding to two additional communities and introducing hygiene kits for every household.
